3. Deposit & Mobilization Phase

  • Pre-Kickoff Cancellation: Clients may cancel a project order within twenty-four (24) hours of initial invoice settlement, provided no technical scoping, architecture setup, repository creation, or wireframing has begun. In this event, a 100% full refund of all amounts paid will be issued, less any direct non-recoverable third-party payment gateway transaction fees incurred via Paystack.

4. Active Development & Milestone Sign-Off

Development occurs in documented iterative sprints. Clients review live functionality on private staging environments. If a client terminates a project midway through active coding, any milestone payments already paid for completed milestones are retained, while any uncommenced milestones are refunded on a pro-rata basis.

Once final project sign-off has occurred, final settlement is cleared, and source code is handed over or published live to the client's production domain:

  • Every custom web platform package includes a 30-day technical warranty following launch. During this period, any technical defects, functional regressions, or bugs attributable to the agreed original scope are resolved promptly at zero additional cost.

6. Monthly Development Retainers

Monthly maintenance retainers (R 1,800/month) operate on a month-to-month basis. A client may cancel their retainer at any time by providing at least fourteen (14) calendar days written notice before their next billing cycle. Fees for the current active billing cycle are non-refundable once the cycle has commenced and engineering hours have been allocated.

All approved refunds are initiated through our official Paystack merchant dashboard back to the original funding source (credit/debit card, Instant EFT bank account).
